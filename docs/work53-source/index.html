How the parts connect

Where rebuilding starts

You can know exactly what needs to change and still live the same Tuesday over again. Maybe a loss has forced you to look. Maybe the bills are paid and the people at your table have learned to talk around the back of your phone. You can start with either one.

Look for one part of the pattern you can answer for. Give it a standard you can keep on an ordinary day. What you actually do with that standard gives you something to stand on the next time life gets hard.

A line gives the day a direction. A workable day lets you repeat the action. Repetition gives your word a record you can believe. As that record changes how you see yourself, an old bargain can lose its hold. You can carry the decision yourself and keep the help you need.

Start where the work is for you. If you already know the line and keep missing it, look at the shape of your day. If you have kept it for a while and still doubt your word, look at what the record can carry.
