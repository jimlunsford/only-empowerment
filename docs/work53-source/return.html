Action, evidence & correction

Return after a miss

What still needs putting right after you tell the truth?

In the rebuilding order

Produce the proof.

See the whole order

A miss tells you something the plan could not. The structure broke, the decision went the wrong way, or somebody is still carrying the cost. Say precisely what happened. Then look at what is still wrong in the world and what you can do about it.

Fictional example

Follow a correction to the people affected

Sima posts an old weekend shift schedule. Two coworkers plan around the wrong start times. She catches the error on Thursday. Follow what the team can actually rely on as the correction develops.

What happened

  1. Thursday, 9:10Sima tells the coordinator she posted the old schedule.
  2. Thursday, 9:35She replaces the schedule, contacts both coworkers and confirms each has the right start time.
  3. The following ThursdayBefore posting, she checks the dated master and sends the current schedule.

What was still open at each point?

Open more than one entry to compare the consequences.

The admission · Who knows

The coordinator now knows the source of the error. The two coworkers still have the old information. An honest admission is one act. The work of reaching the people affected is still open.

The team still cannot rely on the posted times.

The repair · What changed

People who arranged their weekends around the error now have accurate information. One already paid for extra childcare. Sima raises that cost with the coordinator. The schedule correction is complete. The cost has a separate outcome.

Changing the information does not refund the cost or settle how either coworker feels.

The next real posting · What repeated conduct could establish

A planned check has now been used on a real posting. That adds evidence about a change in her routine. It does not make the earlier schedule accurate or establish that there will never be another mistake.

A later set of accurate postings would give the team a longer record to judge.

What carries into your day

An honest admission can change what somebody knows. A completed repair changes something else. An adjusted routine can help you avoid a repeat. Keep those facts separate or you can give yourself credit for work that is still somebody else’s problem.

The original miss belongs in the record alongside whatever you do next. Repeat the return when it matters. Over time, the useful question becomes how reliably you keep the line, notice a miss and correct it.

Say which part you corrected and which consequence is still open. That lets you act again without pretending the first miss disappeared.

In your own life

If someone still holds bad information or carries a cost because of your miss, what could you actually put right? What fact would tell you it reached them?
